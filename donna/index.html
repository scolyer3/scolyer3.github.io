Donna

Donna is a personal assistant tool built and used by one person, Sterling Colyer, on his own computer. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing here to sign up for.

Privacy policy

Donna reads the owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail, read-only, to prepare a daily brief for the owner. It cannot send email, create or move calendar events, or change anything in the owner's Google account.

Everything Donna reads stays on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any third party other than the AI model provider used to write the brief, and no data is sold, shared, or used for advertising. Google user data is used only to show the owner his own calendar and mail, and for no other purpose.

Access can be revoked at any time by the owner at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which removes Donna's stored credentials from further use.
